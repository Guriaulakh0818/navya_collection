## 5. Verified Policies & Operations

- **Logistics Fulfillment**: Shiprocket network across 19,000+ pin codes. Multi-vendor orders dispatch directly from each seller's warehouse/storefront as split consignments.
- **Shipping Rates**: Free delivery on cart orders >₹999; flat ₹99 fee on orders <₹999.
- **Payment Methods**: Razorpay (UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Net Banking) & Cash on Delivery (COD).
- **Return Policy**: 7-day doorstep reverse pickup for unused garments with tags intact. Prepaid refunds initiated within 24 hours of inspection.
- **Merchant Model**: Local clothing stores and boutique owners register at `/become-seller` and manage product listings and order fulfillment.
