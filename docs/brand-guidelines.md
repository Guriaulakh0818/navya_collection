# Navya Collection – Brand Guidelines Document (BGD)

## 1. Purpose
This document defines the official branding standards for Navya Collection.

## 2. Brand Positioning
### Brand Name
Navya Collection

### Industry
Fashion & Apparel

### Current Category
- Gents Garments
- Kids Garments

### Future Expansion
- Women’s Fashion
- Accessories

### Brand Personality
The brand should always feel:
- Modern
- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Family Friendly
- Minimal
- Confident
- Affordable

## 3. Brand Mission
To provide stylish, high-quality and affordable fashion while delivering a premium shopping experience for every customer.

## 4. Brand Vision
To become one of India’s most trusted fashion brands for families through quality products, excellent service, and a memorable shopping experience.

## 5. Brand Values
- Trust
- Quality
- Simplicity
- Customer First
- Transparency
- Consistency

## 6. Brand Voice
All communication should be:
- Friendly
- Clear
- Professional
- Respectful
- Simple
- Positive

## 7. Logo Strategy
The logo consists of two elements:
1. Brand Symbol
2. Wordmark

Both should work independently and together.

## 8. Brand Symbol (To Be Designed)
The symbol must:
- Be original
- Be memorable
- Work in a single color
- Scale well from favicon to signage
- Represent the brand without relying on text
- Be suitable for embroidery, labels, shopping bags, and app icons

Common garment icons such as hangers, shopping bags, or t-shirts should be avoided unless transformed into a unique abstract mark.

## 9. Logo Variations
Will be maintained:
- Primary Logo
- Horizontal Logo
- Stacked Logo
- Icon Only
- Monochrome (Black)
- Monochrome (White)

## 10. Logo Usage Rules
- Maintain clear space around the logo.
- Do not stretch or distort.
- Do not change colors.
- Do not rotate.
- Do not add effects such as shadows or gradients unless officially approved.
- Always use approved brand colors.

## 11. Brand Colors
- Primary: Deep Navy (#183A73)
- Accent: Orange (#F15A25)
- Background: Soft White (#FAFAFA)
- Text: Charcoal (#1E1E1E)

These colors must remain consistent across digital and print materials.

## 12. Typography
- Primary Heading Font: Playfair Display
- Body Font: Poppins

No additional fonts should be introduced without approval.

## 13. Photography Style
Product photography should be:
- High Resolution
- Consistent Lighting
- Clean Background
- Accurate Colors
- Multiple Angles
- Zoom Friendly

Lifestyle images should reflect everyday confidence and comfort.

## 14. Packaging Identity
All packaging should maintain a premium and minimal appearance.

Future branded materials include:
- Courier Poly Mailers
- Thank You Cards
- Product Tags
- Stickers
- Carry Bags
- Gift Boxes

The same color palette and typography must be used throughout.

## 15. Digital Branding
Apply the same identity across:
- Website
- Instagram
- Facebook
- WhatsApp Business
- Google Business Profile
- Email Signatures
- Invoices
- Order Confirmation Emails

## 16. Favicon & App Icon
The favicon must use only the approved brand symbol without text. It should remain identifiable even at 16×16 pixels.

## 17. Brand Consistency Rules
Every visual element must follow the approved:
- Color palette
- Typography
- Icon style
- Spacing system
- Photography standards
- Tone of communication

No unofficial variations should be used.

## 18. Future Brand Assets
The identity system should support:
- Mobile App Icon
- Clothing Labels
- Embroidery Marks
- Social Media Templates
- Exhibition Booths
- Store Signage
- Printed Catalogues
- QR Code Branding

## 19. Brand Approval Checklist
Before releasing any design, verify:
- Correct logo version
- Approved colors
- Correct fonts
- Consistent spacing
- High-quality assets
- Brand tone maintained

## 20. Status
Approved (Foundation)

The only remaining item to be finalized is the official Navya Collection brand symbol, which will become the central visual identity of the brand and will be documented in Version 2.0 of these guidelines.
