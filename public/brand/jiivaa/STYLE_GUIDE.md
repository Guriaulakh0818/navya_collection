## 1. Mascot Profile & Visual DNA (100% Facial Consistency Rules)

To maintain **100% facial and character fidelity** across every page, banner, avatar, notification, and marketing graphic:

### Core Character Identifiers (Visual Anchors):

1. **Face & Eyes**:
   - Stylized chibi/anime young Indian girl with warm wheatish/fair Indian complexion and rosy flushed cheeks.
   - Large, expressive dark-brown anime eyes featuring distinct upper-left circular catchlights and soft lower-iris highlights.
   - Fine arched brows and warm, expressive, smiling mouth.
2. **Hairstyle**:
   - Rich dark brown hair neatly tied in a **high back ponytail** secured with a JiivaaKart orange tie band.
   - Two softly curved, cheek-framing front bangs that hang symmetrically on both sides of her face.
3. **Signature Traditional Jewelry (Earrings)**:
   - **Silver Indian Jhumkas**: A core brand signature. She wears silver bell-shaped jhumkas on **both ears** in EVERY outfit, pose, and expression — whether she is wearing a festive lehenga, casual hoodie, or streetwear track jacket!
4. **Brand Palette**:
   - **Navy Blue (`#0A2342`)**: Conveys trust, premium reliability, and corporate strength.
   - **Brand Orange (`#F28C28`)**: Conveys youth, energy, festivity, and warmth.
   - **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Clean, minimalist modern canvas.
